PeptiSense — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

The short version: PeptiSense does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. Everything you enter — your protocols, doses, vials, and any Apple Health data you import — stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads, and no tracking.

What data the app handles, and where it lives

PeptiSense lets you log peptide protocols and doses, track vials, run a reconstitution calculator, and optionally import your own sleep and heart-rate-variability (HRV) figures from Apple Health. All of this information is stored locally on your device, inside your browser/app storage. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else — in fact, there is no PeptiSense server to upload it to.

Apple Health data

If you choose to import a sleep or HRV export file, that file is read and processed entirely on your device to display your numbers. It is not transmitted anywhere. You can remove it at any time using the Clear button on the Apple Health card, or by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking

The app does not require sign-up, does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not build a profile of you or track your activity across other apps or websites.

Third-party resources

To display correctly, the app loads standard code libraries and fonts from public content-delivery networks (for example unpkg, the Tailwind CDN, and Google Fonts). As part of delivering those files, those providers may receive routine technical information such as your device's IP address and browser type, governed by their own privacy policies. PeptiSense itself does not receive, log, or store any of this.

Deleting your data

Your data is under your control. Use the in-app Clear options, or clear the app/website data in your device settings, to permanently remove everything. Because nothing is stored off-device, deleting it locally deletes it completely.

Children

PeptiSense is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Medical disclaimer

PeptiSense is an informational tracking and calculation tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Peptides and related compounds may be prescription-only or restricted where you live. Always confirm dosing independently and consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting, changing, or stopping any protocol. You are solely responsible for how you use the information this app displays.
